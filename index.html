The 30-day plan

From pound dribble to combo chains

Dribbling only. Every move builds on the ones before it.

Days 1–9Stationary control

Pound dribbles, V-dribbles, wraps, the Maravich pretzel, crossovers, between the legs, behind the back.

Days 10–14On the move

Speed and control dribbles, crossover, hesitation, in-and-out, change of pace.

Days 15–27Legend moves + first combos

Hesi-cross, Skip to My Lou, the killer crossover, Kyrie-style spin, shake and bake.

Days 28–30Combo chains

String it all together, film it, and compare it to your Day 1.

  1. Stance + pound dribble, each hand
  2. Pound ladder: high / middle / low
  3. V-dribbles, front and side
  4. Ball wraps + figure-8 wrap
  5. The Maravich pretzel
  6. Stationary crossover + kill dribble
  7. Stationary between the legs
