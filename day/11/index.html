Crossover (on the move)

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: front crossover, low cross

Change of direction dribble from one hand to the other in front of the body, plant the outside foot, push off.

  1. Step 1Stationary crossover (review)
  2. Step 2Walking crossover at chair
  3. Step 3Jog, plant, crossover
  4. Step 4Full speed crossover, 2 hard dribbles past chair
CuesPlant outside foot; cross low; push off
Kid versionWalking only
