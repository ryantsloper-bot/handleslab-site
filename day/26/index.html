Shake and Bake

 Advanced · Level 4/5

Also called: shake-n-bake around the back

A fake (the shake) into a behind-the-back (the bake) to blow by. Sell the fake with your eyes and shoulders.

  1. Step 1Behind-back (review)
  2. Step 2Ball fake one way (no release) then behind-back
  3. Step 3Walking: shake, bake, 2 dribbles
  4. Step 4Full speed at chair
CuesSell the shake with eyes and shoulders; low; explode
Kid versionSteps 1-2 only
Gear: Ball, chairBuilds on: Behind the back (on the move) (Day 19), In-and-out (Day 13)
