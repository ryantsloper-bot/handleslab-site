Stationary in-and-out

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: one-hand fake crossover, inside-out

Fake a crossover with one hand: push the ball toward the middle, turn the wrist over and bring it back out on the same side. Feet stay put.

  1. Step 1Right hand slow in-and-out x10
  2. Step 2Left hand slow in-and-out x10
  3. Step 3Right hand game speed x10
  4. Step 4Left hand game speed x10
CuesHand on the side of the ball; wrist turns it back out; same hand, no carry
Kid versionSay 'in... and out' out loud; slow and big first
Gear: BallBuilds on: V-dribble, front (side-to-side yo-yo) (Day 3)
