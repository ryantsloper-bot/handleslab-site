Stationary crossover

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: in-place crossover

Bounce the ball from one hand to the other in front of the body, below the knees.

  1. Step 1Cross once, catch with the other hand
  2. Step 2Slow crossovers
  3. Step 3Low fast crossovers
  4. Step 4Wide crossovers (push it outside the hip)
CuesBelow the knees; wide; eyes up
Kid versionSlow first, ball stays low
Gear: BallBuilds on: V-dribble, front (side-to-side yo-yo) (Day 3)

Sources: See The Floor: Dribble Moves glossary · General coaching practice (no single source; standard youth drill)

Kill dribble / rapid low dribble

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: machine gun, low quick dribble

Low, hard one-hand dribbles that finish in quick crossovers.

  1. Step 1Low in-and-out, one hand
  2. Step 2Low pound, straight up and down
  3. Step 3Fastest low pound, ball at the knee
  4. Step 4Low crossovers, hand to hand
CuesTiny bounces; soft knees; fingers
Kid versionTry kneeling first
Gear: BallBuilds on: Pound ladder (high / medium / low) (Day 2)

Sources: General coaching practice (no single source; standard youth drill)
