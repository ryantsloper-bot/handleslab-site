Ricochet / bullet ricochet

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: bullet ricochet

Throw the ball hard between the legs off the floor, catch it behind the back, bring it back and repeat; the bullet version starts overhead.

  1. Step 1Drop ball between legs, catch in front
  2. Step 2Bounce through legs, catch behind back
  3. Step 3Ricochet x10
  4. Step 4Bullet ricochet from overhead
CuesBounce hard; hands ready behind; knees bent
Kid versionSteps 1-2 only
Gear: Ball; 4x4 ftBuilds on: Figure-8 wrap (no dribble) (Day 4)

Figure-8 dribble

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: dribbling figure-8

Dribble around and between the legs in a figure-8.

  1. Step 1Figure-8 wrap (review)
  2. Step 2Figure-8 dribble one way slow
  3. Step 3Reverse
  4. Step 4Fast, low, 20 s
CuesLow dribbles; wide stance
Kid versionSmall slow bounces
Gear: BallBuilds on: Figure-8 wrap (no dribble) (Day 4), Stationary between the legs (Day 7)
