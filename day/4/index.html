Around the world wraps (head, waist, legs)

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: ball wraps

Wrap the ball around the head, waist, then knees without dropping it, both directions.

  1. Step 1Waist wraps x5
  2. Step 2Head wraps x5
  3. Step 3Knee wraps x5
  4. Step 4Head-waist-knees-and-back nonstop
CuesHands only, body still; reverse direction
Kid versionSlow is fine; no dropping
Gear: BallBuilds on: Ball slaps / finger taps (Day 1)

Sources: General coaching practice (no single source; standard youth drill)

Figure-8 wrap (no dribble)

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: figure eight through the legs

Pass the ball around and between the legs in a figure-8 without bouncing.

  1. Step 1Wide stance, wrap right leg
  2. Step 2Wrap left leg
  3. Step 3Full figure-8 slow
  4. Step 4Figure-8 fast, reverse
CuesStay low; quick hands
Kid versionWide stance helps
Gear: BallBuilds on: Around the world wraps (head, waist, legs) (Day 4)

Sources: General coaching practice (no single source; standard youth drill)
