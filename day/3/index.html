V-dribble, front (side-to-side yo-yo)

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: front V, side-to-side yo-yo

One hand dribbles the ball side to side in front of the body in a V shape.

  1. Step 1Right hand front V x10
  2. Step 2Left hand x10
  3. Step 3Faster V
  4. Step 4Low front V (ankle high), each hand
CuesSnap the wrist; off arm up; low
Kid version30 s each hand
Gear: BallBuilds on: Pound dribble (right / left) (Day 1)

Sources: Online Basketball Drills: Yo-Yo Dribbling Drill

V-dribble, side (front-to-back yo-yo)

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: side V

One hand dribbles front to back beside the body.

  1. Step 1Right hand side V x10
  2. Step 2Left hand
  3. Step 3Faster
  4. Step 4Combo: one front V, one side V, repeat
CuesHand rides the ball; stay low
Kid version30 s each hand
Gear: BallBuilds on: V-dribble, front (side-to-side yo-yo) (Day 3)

Sources: Online Basketball Drills: Yo-Yo Dribbling Drill
