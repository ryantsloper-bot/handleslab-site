Front pivot

 Level 1 of 4 · Beginner

Also called: forward pivot

Keep the pivot foot down and step forward around it.

  1. Step 1Pivot right foot, quarter turn
  2. Step 2Half turn
  3. Step 3Full turn
  4. Step 4Pivot both ways with ball
CuesPivot on the ball of the foot; stay low
Kid versionClock game: pivot to 3, 6, 9
Gear: BallBuilds on: Jump stop (Day 10)

Sources: Athletic Officials: So You Think That's A Travel (NFHS rules)

Spin move

 Level 2 of 4 · Intermediate

Also called: reverse, whirl, spin dribble

Plant the inside foot, spin away from the defender pulling the ball tight to the hip, change hands, come out squared. Time it for when the defender leans or reaches.

  1. Step 1Pivot spin holding ball (no dribble)
  2. Step 2Spin dribble walking at chair
  3. Step 3Half-speed spin, land square
  4. Step 4Drive, spin, explode out
CuesPull the ball with you; tight spin; eyes find the rim fast
Kid versionSlow walk-through
Gear: Ball, chairBuilds on: Front pivot (Day 24), Crossover (on the move) (Day 11)

Sources: Bleacher Report: Breaking Down the Signature Moves of NBA's Top Stars (2020) · Championship Productions: Collection of ballhandling drills · Athletic Officials: So You Think That's A Travel (NFHS rules)
